# ADR-0002 — Android as Initial Primary Platform

## Context

The thesis requires launcher/device-management options and inspection/control of existing applications.

## Decision

Use Android as the initial primary platform before iPadOS.

## Consequences

Product and feasibility work target Android behavior first. This does not promise support for every Android device or permanently exclude other platforms.

## Alternatives

iPadOS first and simultaneous multi-platform development—deferred because they weaken focus and device-control access.
