# Style-board iterations

## FINAL — current design system

**Harbour Blue selected by Simon, 2026-09-19.** [Open the final image and one-to-one written design reference](final-harbour-blue/README.md). This is the version to use going forward; it includes the exact board colours, typography, components, visible text and state treatments.

The selected image is copied unchanged from iteration 5 / 04. Earlier rounds below are preserved as history, not competing recommendations.

**Read [Simon's Notes - style boards](<Notes - style boards.md>) before every new style-board round.** This is his ongoing feedback notebook. Keep it here and preserve his wording; put generated boards, gallery and prompts in their own numbered iteration folder.

## Iteration 5 — Harbour Blue selected

The shortlist is resolved: **04 — Harbour Blue** is selected. The recommendation and pending-choice wording below record the earlier comparison only.

**Linen canvas, white surface and dark ink are now selected.** [Four action/accent variations](iteration-5/README.md) compare Plum + Iris, Plum + Cobalt, Periwinkle + Cobalt and Harbour Blue. Soft Plum remains the baseline; Simon explicitly authorized blue alternatives. All share a clear ordinary outline, a separate stronger focus ring and red Stop.

Recommend **02 — Plum + Cobalt**, with **03 — Periwinkle + Cobalt** as the direct challenger. Simon's next choice should establish the main board; the recommendation is not acceptance.

## Iteration 4 — Linen canvas selected

Simon selected Linen rather than Pearl/Defined for the canvas, retained surface/ink, and liked Clear Send's blue-ish direction. Continue with iteration 5; earlier proposals below are historical.

**Simon selected Soft Plum as the direction to move forward with.** [Three close refinements](iteration-4/README.md) explore a subtly warmer off-white canvas and more expressive same-family plum outlines: Pearl, Linen and Defined. The white chat box, plum buttons, type and layout stay fixed. New canvas/outline shades remain open for review.

## Iteration 3 — Soft Plum selected

Iteration 4 also includes a fourth **Clear Send** study responding to the live notebook: Pearl with a more vivid violet Send action. This is a focused action-emphasis option, not a replacement identity.

The historical neutral-foundation presets were Graphite, Soft Plum, Dusky Violet and Clay. They shared the same warm-grey canvas, white chat box/panel, typography and layout; only small action accents changed. The iteration-3 intermediate folder is not retained in this checkout; its session record preserves the handoff description.

Simon's latest chat feedback rejects iteration 2's overly colored combinations, especially the chat-box/background pairing. A stable canvas remains promising, and multiple user-selectable presets are worth exploring. These are appearance studies, not implemented settings or a final default.

## Iteration 2 — archived, color direction rejected

The historical iteration-2 boards were Apricot & Ink, Lilac & Vermilion, Cocoa & Rose and Ink & Tangerine. That intermediate folder is not retained in this checkout; its session record preserves the handoff description.

The brief from his iteration 1 feedback: less hospital/health-tech association; no green/blue/yellow brand palette; warmer backgrounds with more contrasting personality; more distinctive type. Preserve the rounded composer and its colored states, take Living Pages' blue focus treatment, and use Bright Signal's context-panel structure.

## Iteration 1 — archived

[Open the first four boards](iteration-1/README.md): Open Day, Bright Signal, Living Pages and Everyday Spark. Original images and prompts are preserved together. Everyday Spark was Simon's favorite of this set, but none of its palette or type was selected.
