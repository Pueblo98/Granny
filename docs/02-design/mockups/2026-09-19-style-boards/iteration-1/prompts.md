# Generation record

Built-in image generation; no CLI/API fallback. Four initial boards were regenerated as edits to remove obscuring dark shading. Open Day, Living Pages and Everyday Spark received a second targeted typography correction. The final four deliverables are embedded in the [gallery](README.md); rejected drafts remain outside the project. Each prompt below was submitted verbatim. Colors/fonts are proposals; image labels and pixels do not override the canonical brand text.

## 01 / Open Day

### Initial generation

```text
Use case: ui-mockup. Create one beautifully art-directed, high-resolution landscape visual design-system STYLE TILE / FOUNDATION BOARD for Granny, a codename for a conversation-first Android tablet app for independent older adults. This is a flat graphic board, not a photo of a board or a device render. Sophisticated approachable adult typography, generous whitespace, precise legible text, no glossy AI aesthetic. This set compares color and type on IDENTICAL composition. Title at top left, small "GRANNY / STYLE EXPLORATION / 19 SEPT 2026" above. Top half has a color section with six large swatches and exact role names/HEX captions, a five-tone primary shade strip, and a typography specimen with specified family names, Aa, "What's on your mind?", "A little help with everyday things.", and "Il1 O0 0123456789". Label type scale "Display 32/40 · Body 20/30 · Label 20/28". Bottom half shows a wide normal-size chat composer, a compact composer, and ONE optional context panel. Composer is a full smoothly rounded speech balloon with short lower-left tail, dark thin outline and pastel tint; large readable "What's on your mind?", visible persistent label "Type a request", labeled microphone "Talk" and labeled arrow "Send". Compact repeats same silhouette. Context panel copy exactly "Draft waiting" / "Continue when you're ready." / "Open draft" / "Hide", no personal content. Also a small component-state strip labeled "Default", "Focus", "Pressed", "Stop"; focus has a visible offset ring and Stop a square icon with text. These are component specimens, not a busy Home dashboard. Footer "Proposed palette & type · Simulated UI · Font rendering is illustrative". Use text crisply, spell all HEX codes correctly. No mascots, flowers, elderly caricatures, sparkles, gradients, glass, texture behind text, logos, photography or extra widgets. Maintain same layout and copy for all directions.
Title (verbatim): "01 / Open Day".
Style: Clear, humane, fresh green and warm light. Humanist sans with open counters. Font display and body: Atkinson Hyperlegible Next; display 700, body 400, labels 600.
Exact colors: Canvas #F5FAF7; Surface #FFFFFF; Ink #152522; Muted #44524C; Primary #123B36; Accent #F4B942. Primary shade strip light to dark: #E8F2EE #C8DDD4 #86B3A2 #275D54 #123B36. Composer tint #E8F2EE and dark #123B36 outline. Focus #5C32A3. Stop outline #982D35.
```

### Readability revision

```text
Edit this style board to fix a critical readability problem: REMOVE ALL BLACK SHADOWS, dark cloudy gradient overlays, vignette, atmospheric haze, glow, blur, spotlights and lighting effects across the ENTIRE IMAGE. Replace the board background with one perfectly flat, uniform, solid very light color from edge to edge, including all top sections. Canvas #F5FAF7; Surface #FFFFFF; Ink #152522; Muted #44524C; Primary #123B36; Accent #F4B942. Primary shade strip light to dark: #E8F2EE #C8DDD4 #86B3A2 #275D54 #123B36. Composer tint #E8F2EE and dark #123B36 outline. Focus #5C32A3. Stop outline #982D35. Every title and font specimen must become fully visible as crisp dark ink on the light background, with no dark patch behind it. The result must look like a flat printed design specification sheet viewed straight on, never a cinematic render. Preserve palette labels, HEX codes, font names, UI copy and board structure. Make the chat shapes much rounder with fully pill-rounded ends and a short soft lower-left tail, exactly the Round conversation silhouette. Both composers must keep "Type a request", "What's on your mind?", "Talk", "Send". Body/input text is sans serif even for Living Pages; serif only for display headline specimens. Component-state strip must be simple standalone button specimens Default / Focus / Pressed / Stop rather than mini forms; the last is one square icon plus visible "Stop", no other controls in that specimen. Focus ring visibly separated from control. No new slogans. Footer "Proposed palette & type · Simulated UI · Font rendering is illustrative".
```

### Typography correction

```text
Correct ONLY the typography on this otherwise good flat style board. EVERY letter on this entire green board must be humanist SANS SERIF, including the large sample "What's on your mind?" and "Il1 O0 0123456789". Remove all serifs from those two lines. The specified font is Atkinson Hyperlegible Next. Do not change colors, layout, labels, UI, shapes, or light background. No shadows or gradients.
```
