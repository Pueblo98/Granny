# Shared conversation state pack — exact generation prompts

Each built-in image-generation call produced one full-screen raster. These are
the exact revised prompts recorded by the generator. In every prompt, Image 1,
Image 2 and Image 3 refer respectively to the immediately named composition,
Harbour Blue and shell references supplied during generation.

## 01 — Listening

```text
Use case: ui-mockup
Asset type: Listening state, frame 1 of a shared Granny conversation-state pack
Input images: Image 1 is the selected full-screen Home composition and controls overall shell, spacing and Round composer; Image 2 is the exact Harbour Blue visual authority; Image 3 demonstrates the spacious conversation treatment and is style reference only.
Primary request: create a polished full-screen 16:10 landscape tablet mockup showing explicit microphone capture after the person starts Talk. Preserve the calm Granny shell: small "Granny" at upper left and written "Menu" at upper right. Make the central attention a large heading "Listening" and supporting line "Say what you would like to do." Beneath it, show an expanded Round conversation speech-balloon surface near the lower half. Inside it use persistent label "Heard so far" and provisional transcript "Tell David I’ll call…" with a visible caret. Clearly show an outlined microphone with written state "Listening". Provide large written controls "Done listening", "Cancel", and quiet route "Type instead". Done listening is the one Harbour Blue primary action; Cancel is outlined. The provisional transcript is visibly provisional through the written label, not low contrast alone.
Text (verbatim): "Granny", "Menu", "Listening", "Say what you would like to do.", "Heard so far", "Tell David I’ll call…", "Listening", "Done listening", "Cancel", "Type instead".
Style/medium: realistic high-fidelity product UI mockup, adult, warm, calm, candid and accessible; Bricolage Grotesque-like display heading, DM Sans-like body and controls.
Composition/framing: full-screen representative 16:10 landscape tablet without a device frame; generous margins, a wide quiet reading field, no zoomed-in crop; expanded composer remains fully visible with space for a separated focus ring.
Color palette: Canvas #FBF6EE, Surface #FFFFFF, Ink #2E2D32, Accent #2C5981, Outline #597DA0, Focus ring #4930A1 only for focus, Stop/danger #962F43 only for genuine Stop/danger, On-colour #FFFFFF.
Constraints: this is explicit simulated capture, not always-on listening. No waveform-only meaning, pulsing orb, decorative sound bars, animation, AI sparkle, gradient, glass, glow, shadow depth, avatar, person, room art, private data, bottom navigation, icon-only control, watermark or extra copy. Do not show Send or Stop in this listening state.
```
