# Context Room assets — iteration 1

This completed mockup round applies Simon's approved Kitchen direction across
the six starter rooms. It contains **84 selected raster studies**: one mark,
portrait, backdrop, transparent decor cluster, motif, transparent empty state
and eight direct-browse symbols per room. These are review assets, not accepted
production art or SVG masters.

The shell reference is the selected
[Harbour Blue board](https://github.com/Pueblo98/Granny/blob/4c2a7bc0a8dd9684d48e290d7bda80716ae5e587/docs/02-design/mockups/2026-09-19-style-boards/final-harbour-blue/README.md)
and its unchanged source image,
`docs/02-design/mockups/2026-09-19-style-boards/final-harbour-blue/harbour-blue-final.png`.
The exact common rules are in the [visual bible](visual-bible.md); each room's
initial prompts, symbol construction and corrections are in its linked prompt
record. The [manifest](manifest.json) records every selected path, actual
dimensions, alpha state and file-level review status.

## Structural thesis

All six rooms belong to one calm, carefully kept world. A room is recognized by
its written name in the product plus a distinct threshold silhouette and a few
dossier-owned objects. The product shell keeps Linen and Harbour Blue semantics;
room colors are restrained, nonsemantic reinforcement. Backdrops reserve a large
quiet content field, while decor, motifs and empty-state art can disappear
without removing navigation or meaning.

## Deliberate omissions

No people, mascots, assistant personalities, controls, notification counts,
status claims, real documents, private data, commercial logos, legible titles,
prices, progress metrics or baked navigation text appear. The round does not
generate pack 02–08, a Rooms UI, room interiors as implemented screens, frontend
code, Android resources, Figma frames or production vector masters.

## Cross-room review

| Room | Structural thesis | Strongest concern | Corrective retries |
|---|---|---|---:|
| Kitchen | A familiar pantry threshold makes Kitchen readable without turning it into a separate assistant. | Backdrop and decor are slightly more realistic than the softer portrait; mark shading remains. | 3 |
| Fitness / Movement | An open, body-free morning movement space signals readiness without metrics or gym pressure. | Mat-sun mark is abstract at 64px; transparent illustrated cutouts retain a soft halo. | 4 |
| Trips | A modest open case and threshold make planning recognizable without assuming flying or luxury. | Portrait leans toward a Mediterranean setting; cutouts retain a soft halo. | 2 |
| Garden | Gate and broad rows give the clearest spatial identity while staying usable for a community plot. | Portrait has more environmental detail than the shared compact vignettes; cutouts retain a soft halo. | 2 |
| Reading | A window seat and open book create comfort without making dense library decor the interface. | Mark is more detailed than the family; cutouts retain a soft halo. | 3 |
| Projects / Hobbies | An empty worktable is neutral enough to support any chosen project without inferred identity. | The very neutral mark depends strongly on its written label; cutouts retain a soft halo. | 2 |

The six marks remain distinct at both 64px and 96px on Linen. All 48 collection
symbols remain visually distinct at 96px when paired with their required written
labels. Portraits remain recognizable at small Home/library scale. Every selected
backdrop leaves a quiet central field. Removing motifs, decor and empty-state art
leaves written room identity, direct browse/search and navigation conceptually
intact.

## Fidelity limits and unrun checks

- Image generation produced varying native raster dimensions; implementation
  still needs deliberate Android crops, density resources and safe-area tests.
- Marks and symbols are transparent raster studies with slight tonal shading,
  not uniform-color SVG masters.
- Decor and empty-state PNGs have real alpha, but the five rooms generated after
  Kitchen retain a visible soft halo after one targeted correction. They should
  be cleaned or regenerated before production placement.
- Motifs were visually reviewed as subtle tiles, but seamless repetition is
  unproven.
- The images do not prove contrast, Android dp targets, TalkBack, keyboard order,
  200% text reflow, older-adult comprehension, cultural fit or user preference.
- Simon's approval of the Kitchen direction authorized this continuation; it
  does not select final production art. Iteration 1 remains open for review.
