# Fitness F01 — generation prompts

These are the exact built-in image-generation prompts for F01 **Morning
Stretch**, paired with **F-M01 Mat sunrise**. Every call produces one asset.

## F-M01 room mark

```text
Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: Fitness Context Room mark raster study, transparent PNG
Shared visual bible: adult, calm, original, vector-friendly mark for Granny's Harbour Blue system; bold rounded silhouette, clean antialiased edges, consistent visual weight with the Kitchen open-cupboard mark; uniform solid Ink #2E2D32.
Primary request: F-M01 Mat sunrise: one rolled movement mat below one broad rising semicircle, unified as a simple emblem.
Composition: centered square with generous even padding, readable at 64px and 96px.
Constraints: genuine transparent alpha; no people, body, yoga-only spiritual cue, tiny sun rays, text, letters, badge, gradient, lighting, texture, shadow, 3D, logo or watermark.
```

## Collection symbols

Every symbol uses this common construction: original one-color vector-friendly
flat raster study, uniform Ink `#2E2D32`, bold rounded silhouette, transparent
square canvas, generous padding, readable at 96px, no text/status/action badge,
gradient, lighting, texture, shadow, 3D, logo or watermark.

- **Routines:** two nested blank rounded routine cards with one broad offset edge; no writing lines or checkmarks.
- **Walking:** two broad path curves meeting one quiet horizon line; no footsteps, person, pin or step count.
- **Classes:** exactly three simple rolled mats aligned beneath one shallow roof/room arc; no people or timetable text.
- **Strength:** two light rounded dumbbells balanced on one broad baseline; no bodybuilder, trophy or heavy-gym styling.
- **Stretching:** one unrolled mat crossed by one broad open movement arc; no body or yoga-spiritual icon.
- **Cycling:** one bold bicycle wheel arc beside a short stable ground line; no brand, speed lines or race cue.
- **Swimming:** exactly three broad horizontal ripple/lane bands inside one softly rounded frame; no medal or warning waves.
- **Equipment notes:** one simple open equipment bag beside one completely blank note card; no brand, power symbol or warning.

## Review record

All 14 selected outputs, actual dimensions, alpha state and file-level review
notes are recorded in [the iteration manifest](../manifest.json). The selected
mark, decor, motif and empty state each followed one targeted correction. The
mark removed rays; the motif reduced contrast; the cutout corrections explicitly
removed backdrops and shadows. Both cutouts have real RGBA transparency but
still show a soft alpha halo. All eight symbols were inspected together at
96px and remain distinct beside written labels.
