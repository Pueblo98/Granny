# Garden prompts

Every prompt inherits the shared visual bible. G01 **Morning Allotment** and
G-M04 **Garden rows** are the dossier-owned sources. Plants remain generic and
the setting does not assume property ownership, mobility or climate.

## Room assets

- **Mark:** Original vector-friendly flat emblem: three broad garden rows inside one soft horizon frame. Bold rounded silhouette, uniform solid Ink #2E2D32, genuine transparent alpha, generous square padding, readable at 64px. No farm scale, crop chart, text, gradient, lighting, texture, shadow, 3D, logo or watermark.
- **Portrait:** Open simple garden gate looking toward broad planted rows and a soft morning sky. Soft matte editorial gouache, broad rounded generic plant forms, restrained sage and terracotta compatible with Linen and Harbour Blue, diffuse light, generous breathing room. No people, estate, labels, tools, forecast, text, logo or watermark.
- **Backdrop:** Sheltered modest planning shed with garden daylight, pale materials, edge storage and a large quiet central wall for content. Soft matte editorial gouache, same camera height and finish as the room family. No people, dense flowers, diagnosis, pesticide, text, controls, gradient, logo or watermark.
- **Decor:** Transparent cluster of one seed tray, one pair of soft garden gloves and one unlabelled rounded watering can. Matte editorial gouache, broad forms, sage and terracotta accents, generous transparent margin. No surface, halo, shadow, plants, sharp tools, text, logo or watermark.
- **Motif:** Very subtle broad garden-row lines on Linen #FBF6EE, sparse, low contrast and nonsemantic. No crop chart, scene, text, border, gradient, depth, logo or watermark.
- **Empty state:** Transparent illustration of one empty seed tray beside one completely blank plant card. Matte editorial gouache, broad simple forms and generous transparent space. No seeds, writing, plant, surface, halo, shadow, text, logo or watermark.

## Collection symbols

All symbols use one original vector-friendly flat construction: uniform solid
Ink #2E2D32, bold rounded silhouette, consistent medium weight, genuine
transparent alpha, generous square padding, readable at 64px and 96px. No
text, badges, gradients, lighting, texture, shadows, 3D, logos or watermarks.

- **Plants:** broad pot with a sturdy two-leaf generic seedling.
- **Watering:** rounded unlabelled watering can with one broad water arc, no reminder badge.
- **Seasonal plans:** four broad leaf shapes around one blank page, no weather icons.
- **Photos:** two overlapping blank photo frames with one generic leaf silhouette.
- **Seeds:** open plain seed packet with three large seed shapes, no label.
- **Supplies:** simple soft-sided garden tote with one glove and one rounded scoop.
- **Things to do:** blank task sheet beside one broad garden row, no checkmark.
- **Garden journal:** closed rounded notebook with one simple leaf inset, no writing.

## Review record

All 14 selected outputs, actual dimensions, alpha state and file-level review
notes are recorded in [the iteration manifest](../manifest.json). Decor and
empty-state calls each received one targeted correction explicitly removing
the generated backdrop, surface and shadow. Their selected PNGs have real RGBA
transparency but retain a soft alpha halo. The gate/rows portrait and G-M04 mark
remain especially clear in the small-scale comparison.
