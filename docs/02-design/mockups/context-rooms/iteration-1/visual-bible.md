# Context Rooms iteration 1 visual bible

This visual bible is fixed for iteration 1. It governs the Kitchen checkpoint
and, after Simon's review, the remaining five first-pack room identities. It is
a generation constraint, not approval of production art.

The selected shell source inspected before generation was
`docs/02-design/mockups/2026-09-19-style-boards/final-harbour-blue/harbour-blue-final.png`.
That reference remains an input and is not modified by this iteration.

## Shared art narrative

All rooms belong to one calm, carefully kept world illustrated by one artist.
The family is adult, familiar, quietly optimistic and ready to use. Use soft
matte editorial illustration, broad rounded forms, restrained tactile
materials, diffuse natural light, gentle edge softness and generous empty
space. Simplify objects enough to read at tablet-thumbnail scale while
retaining material cues. Avoid both sterile architectural rendering and cute
clip-art character.

Rooms differ through threshold silhouette, key objects, material and object
grouping—not color alone. Portraits and backdrops use a consistent slightly
elevated adult eye-level camera, modest depth, soft shadows and simplified
perspective. Decor and empty states use the same object construction and light.

## Relationship to Harbour Blue

The product shell remains Linen `#FBF6EE`, Surface `#FFFFFF`, Ink `#2E2D32`,
Accent `#2C5981` and Outline `#597DA0`. Room art may echo Linen, warm ceramic,
light wood and a restrained herb/sage family for Kitchen, but room color is
nonsemantic. Do not imitate the Send, focus, Stop, warning or confirmation
roles. No decorative element carries UI meaning.

## Materials and lighting

- Matte warm-white plaster, pale natural wood, unglazed or softly glazed
  ceramic, woven linen and restrained living herbs.
- Diffuse morning daylight from one plausible window direction.
- Broad soft-edged shadows only; no dramatic spotlight, glow, bloom, glass
  effects, gradients or glossy luxury styling.
- Subtle hand-painted texture is allowed, but no high-frequency grain behind
  reading areas.

## Perspective and safe space

- Portrait: one clear threshold or compact vignette, readable at 192px, with a
  bold outer silhouette and no miniature floor plan.
- Backdrop: wide 16:10 scene with low detail through the middle 60%; important
  objects remain at the edges and away from composer/content/Stop zones.
- Decor and empty state: isolated, balanced still-life clusters with generous
  transparent margin and no surface-dependent cast shadow.
- Motif: broad, seamless and extremely low contrast; safe to remove entirely.

## Mark and collection-symbol construction

Use one-color, vector-friendly raster studies with a strong outer silhouette,
rounded corner character and consistent visual weight. Prefer a bold filled
form with one simple negative-space/internal idea. Marks must survive at 64px;
symbols at 96px. Center on a genuinely transparent canvas with generous,
consistent padding. No letters, labels, emoji, app-badge container, tiny scene,
fine detail, status mark or action cue. These PNGs are studies, not SVG masters.

## Shared exclusions

No people, faces, hands, mascots, separate room assistants, childish cartoon
styling, clinical or therapeutic cues, nostalgia caricature, luxury staging,
commercial logo, readable packaging, watermark, baked text, counts,
notifications, fake documents, progress/completion, AI glow, gradients, glass
effects, flames or focal knives. Do not imitate a living artist.

Kitchen additionally avoids domestic-role assumptions, culturally assumed
cuisine, dirty dishes and alcohol emphasis. Every asset must remain useful when
converted to grayscale, and removing decor must never remove room recognition.
