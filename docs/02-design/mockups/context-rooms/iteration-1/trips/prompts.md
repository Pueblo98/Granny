# Trips prompts

Every prompt below inherits the shared visual bible. T01 **Light Packing** and
T-M01 **Open suitcase** are the dossier-owned sources. The fictional artwork
contains no real destination, booking, document or personal data.

## Room assets

- **Mark:** Original vector-friendly flat emblem for Trips: a rounded suitcase with one open-lid arc. Bold rounded silhouette, uniform solid Ink #2E2D32, genuine transparent alpha, generous square padding, readable at 64px. No stickers, contents, airline cues, text, gradient, lighting, texture, shadow, 3D, logo or watermark.
- **Portrait:** Soft matte editorial illustration of a half-open modest suitcase on a bench inside a broad welcoming threshold. Adult, familiar, quiet, simplified rounded forms, restrained dusty blue and warm ochre accents compatible with Linen #FBF6EE, diffuse natural daylight, generous empty space, no people, destination, labels, text, logos or watermark.
- **Backdrop:** Wide calm trip-planning room in soft matte editorial illustration: Linen canvas, clear simple table, soft daylight, broad rounded architecture, and a large quiet central content region. Same camera height and finish as the shared room family. No people, maps, destinations, signs, bookings, text, controls, gradients, logos or watermark.
- **Decor:** Transparent isolated decor cluster: one modest half-open case, one folded scarf and one completely blank checklist card. Soft matte editorial gouache, broad rounded forms, restrained dusty blue and warm ochre, generous transparent margin. No surface, halo, cast shadow, people, writing, tickets, brands, text, gradient, glossy 3D or watermark.
- **Motif:** Broad-scale, very subtle wide luggage-strap bands on Linen #FBF6EE, sparse and low contrast, removable and nonsemantic. Keep most of the tile visually quiet. No buckle focal point, text, scene, border, gradient, shadow, logo or watermark.
- **Empty state:** Transparent empty-state illustration of one open modest case beside one completely blank list. Soft matte editorial gouache, simple rounded forms, generous transparent space. No contents, writing, destination, ticket, people, surface, halo, cast shadow, text, gradient, logo or watermark.

## Collection symbols

All eight symbols use one original vector-friendly flat construction: uniform
solid Ink #2E2D32, bold rounded silhouette, consistent medium weight, genuine
transparent alpha, generous square padding, and readability at 64px and 96px.
No text, letters, numbers, badges, gradients, lighting, texture, shadows, 3D,
logos or watermarks.

- **Packing lists:** open suitcase beside one blank list sheet.
- **Reservations:** two overlapping blank reservation slips with one simple calendar notch, no checkmark.
- **Places:** broad doorway opening toward one simple horizon, no map pin.
- **Travel documents:** generic open document wallet with blank inserts, never a passport crest.
- **Day plans:** blank day-page shape beside a simple sun arc, no clock or date.
- **Transport notes:** simple train window beside one blank note card, no operator styling.
- **People to visit:** two neutral house silhouettes linked by one gentle path, no faces.
- **Past trips:** closed suitcase beside one plain archive box, no stickers or completion mark.

## Review record

All 14 selected outputs, actual dimensions, alpha state and file-level review
notes are recorded in [the iteration manifest](../manifest.json). Decor and
empty-state calls each received one targeted correction: an empty case was
required, and the correction explicitly removed backdrop, vignette, surface,
glow, halo and cast shadow. Their selected PNGs have real RGBA transparency,
but a soft halo remains. The portrait, mark and all eight symbols remained
recognizable in the small-scale review.
