# Projects prompts

Every prompt inherits the shared visual bible. P01 **Open Worktable** and P-M01
**Open worktable** are the neutral dossier-owned sources. No particular hobby,
profession, gender, skill or spending level is inferred.

## Room assets

- **Mark:** Broad table line beneath one open rounded arch. Flat Ink #2E2D32 silhouette, genuine transparent alpha, generous square padding and 64px readability. No office desk, tool, text, gradient, shadow, logo or watermark.
- **Portrait:** Broad welcoming threshold with one clear worktable and one simple empty tray. Soft matte editorial gouache, warm neutral materials, restrained Harbour Blue accent and generous space. No specific hobby, people, tools, writing, text or watermark.
- **Backdrop:** Warm-neutral project room with a very large empty work surface and quiet wall. Soft matte editorial gouache, broad rounded forms and restrained edge storage. No specific hobby, tools behind content, progress board, text, controls, gradient or watermark.
- **Decor:** Transparent cluster of one plain pencil cup, one empty tray and one completely blank notebook. Matte editorial gouache, broad forms and generous transparent margin. No sharp tools, writing, surface, halo, shadow, text or watermark.
- **Motif:** Very subtle sparse measurement-free grid on Linen #FBF6EE, low contrast and nonsemantic. No ruler numbers, measurements, graph-paper density, text, border, gradient or watermark.
- **Empty state:** Transparent illustration of one empty tray beside one completely blank project sheet. Matte editorial gouache, broad simple forms and generous transparent space. No tool, writing, surface, halo, shadow, text or watermark.

## Collection symbols

All symbols use one original vector-friendly flat construction: uniform solid
Ink #2E2D32, bold rounded silhouette, consistent medium weight, genuine
transparent alpha, generous square padding, readable at 64px and 96px. No
text, badges, gradients, lighting, texture, shadows, 3D, logos or watermarks.

- **Plans:** two overlapping blank plan sheets with one broad folded corner.
- **Reference images:** three overlapping blank image frames, no camera.
- **Notes:** blank notebook beside one broad pencil form, no writing.
- **Materials:** open neutral storage tray with three simple generic parts.
- **Drafts:** three offset blank page silhouettes with one broad revision arrow.
- **Things to do:** blank task page beside one small tray, no checkmark.
- **Recently used:** open tray beside one gentle recency arc, no clock or status.
- **Archive:** closed archive box with one blank project sheet, no completion mark.

## Review record

All 14 selected outputs, actual dimensions, alpha state and file-level review
notes are recorded in [the iteration manifest](../manifest.json). Decor and
empty-state calls each received one targeted correction explicitly removing
backdrop, vignette, surface and shadow. Their selected PNGs have real RGBA
transparency but retain a soft halo. The neutral table/arch mark, portrait and
backdrop avoid assigning a hobby, profession, gender or skill level.
