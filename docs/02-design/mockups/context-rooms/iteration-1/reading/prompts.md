# Reading prompts

Every prompt inherits the shared visual bible. R01 **Window Seat** and R-M07
**Reading window** are the dossier-owned sources. The art includes no real
title, author, quote, source or progress state.

## Room assets

- **Mark:** Broad rounded window frame with one simple open book on its sill. Flat Ink #2E2D32 silhouette, genuine transparent alpha, generous square padding and 64px readability. No landscape detail, page lines, text, gradient, shadow, logo or watermark.
- **Portrait:** Wide window threshold with broad book and cushion shapes, diffuse daylight and generous quiet space. Soft matte editorial gouache, restrained dusty blue and muted amber on Linen, adult and uncluttered. No people, titles, quotes, dense shelves, text or watermark.
- **Backdrop:** Bright reading room with an edge window, pale wall and a large protected central field. Soft matte editorial gouache, broad rounded forms and restrained detail. No people, shelf wall, title, progress, text, controls, gradient or watermark.
- **Decor:** Transparent cluster of one blank closed book, one glasses case and one broad cushion. Matte editorial gouache, simplified forms and generous transparent margin. No glasses prescription claim, title, surface, halo, shadow, text or watermark.
- **Motif:** Very subtle broad page-corner curves on Linen #FBF6EE, sparse, low contrast and nonsemantic. No words, book scene, border, gradient, depth or watermark.
- **Empty state:** Transparent illustration of one blank closed book and one blank bookmark. Matte editorial gouache, broad simple forms and generous transparent space. No title, progress, surface, halo, shadow, text or watermark.

## Collection symbols

All symbols use one original vector-friendly flat construction: uniform solid
Ink #2E2D32, bold rounded silhouette, consistent medium weight, genuine
transparent alpha, generous square padding, readable at 64px and 96px. No
text, badges, gradients, lighting, texture, shadows, 3D, logos or watermarks.

- **Books:** one open book plus one small speaker arc to include spoken reading.
- **Articles:** two overlapping blank page shapes, one portrait and one landscape.
- **Currently reading:** open book beside one simple continuation arrow, no percentage.
- **Reading list:** three broad blank book blocks beside one blank list page.
- **Notes:** blank note page beside one broad pencil shape, no writing.
- **Book club:** three closed book shapes around one simple round table, no people.
- **Finished:** closed book beside one simple archive tray, no checkmark or trophy.
- **Sources:** open book linked to one generic browser-page outline, no publisher mark.

## Review record

All 14 selected outputs, actual dimensions, alpha state and file-level review
notes are recorded in [the iteration manifest](../manifest.json). The first
backdrop was rejected for dense old-library clutter and replaced with a sparse
Linen wall, edge window seat and small table. Decor and empty-state calls each
received one transparency correction; their PNGs have real RGBA transparency
but retain a soft halo. The portrait contains only non-legible page texture.
