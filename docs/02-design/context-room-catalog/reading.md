# Reading room asset dossier

## Room definition

**Job:** find, resume and discuss books, articles and saved reading while
keeping source, progress and accessibility settings in semantic UI. Reading is
not a decorative library fantasy and does not assume print, perfect vision or
a particular literary taste.

**Suggested purpose copy:** “Books, articles and things you want to read.”

## Eight room-mark ideas

| ID | Mark concept | Silhouette and meaning | Avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| R-M01 | Open-book doorway | Two broad pages forming an open threshold | Fine page lines or school textbook badge |
| R-M02 | Bookmark window | Rounded page shape with one bold bookmark notch | Ribbon that looks like an award |
| R-M03 | Lamp and page | Reading-lamp arc over one simple page | Task-light control or tiny bulb detail |
| R-M04 | Book stack arch | Three broad book blocks forming a soft arch | Long stack, titles or academic prestige cue |
| R-M05 | Chair and book | Stable chair silhouette with open book shape | Frailty/retirement stereotype or cozy clutter |
| R-M06 | Turning page | One page corner turning into a doorway curve | Motion-dependent meaning |
| R-M07 | Reading window | Broad window frame with one open book on sill | Landscape scene too detailed at small size |
| R-M08 | Shelf opening | One shelf and two large book blocks inside a frame | Dense library grid or store logo |

**Recommended neutral mark:** R-M01. **Comfort alternative:** R-M03.

## Direct-browse symbol kit

Generate: **Books**, **Articles**, **Currently reading**, **Reading list**,
**Notes**, **Book club**, **Finished** and **Sources**. Progress percentages,
font controls and source details are live UI, not part of the symbols or art.

## Generation notes

No legible titles, quotes, author names, publisher marks, fake progress,
bookstore branding or magical-book effects. Avoid floor-to-ceiling shelf
clutter and “old study” stereotypes. Reading may be print, digital or spoken;
do not make a paper book the only semantic indicator. Large-print comfort is a
clear visual option, not an assumption about the person.
