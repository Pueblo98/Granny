# Trips room asset dossier

## Room definition

**Job:** collect packing lists, reservations, destination notes and saved places
so a person can plan or resume a trip without treating the room as a booking
service. Trips may cover a day visit, family stay, train journey or flight; the
art must not assume wealth, nationality or international travel.

**Suggested purpose copy:** “Plans, lists and useful details for going away.”

## Eight room-mark ideas

| ID | Mark concept | Silhouette and meaning | Avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| T-M01 | Open suitcase | Rounded case with one open-lid arc | Airline branding, stickers or tiny contents |
| T-M02 | Path and horizon | Broad path tapering toward one horizon line | Map-pin cliché or navigation promise |
| T-M03 | Train window | Rounded window with one passing-landscape band | Specific rail operator styling |
| T-M04 | Compass arc | Partial compass ring with one clear directional wedge | Fine compass lettering or “always knows” implication |
| T-M05 | Folded map | Three broad folds forming an open doorway | Legible roads, labels or real location data |
| T-M06 | Luggage tag | Soft tag silhouette with one blank inset | Personal name/address or retail price tag |
| T-M07 | Air window | Rounded window with cloud/horizon shapes | Airline-only default or aircraft logo |
| T-M08 | Open doorway | Doorframe opening toward one distant landmark shape | National flags or recognizable private location |

**Recommended neutral mark:** T-M02. **Practical alternative:** T-M01.

## Direct-browse symbol kit

Generate: **Packing lists**, **Reservations**, **Places**, **Travel documents**,
**Day plans**, **Transport notes**, **People to visit** and **Past trips**.
“Travel documents” uses a generic wallet, not a passport crest. Real booking
status, dates and addresses remain semantic text protected by privacy rules.

## Generation notes

No flags, visa/passport replicas, airline logos, real tickets, legible maps,
booking prices, luxury-resort assumptions or fabricated confirmation states.
Keep transport variety so Trips does not mean flying. Do not bake a specific
destination into the generic pack; destination photos belong to content and
retain source/privacy metadata.
