# Projects and Hobbies room asset dossier

## Room definition

**Job:** gather notes, plans, reference images, drafts and parts for something
the person has chosen to make or complete. Because “Projects” is broad, the
assistant should recommend a pack only from the person's stated activity. It
must not infer gender, skill, profession or spending level.

**Suggested purpose copy:** “Plans, references and things you are working on.”

## Eight room-mark ideas

| ID | Mark concept | Silhouette and meaning | Avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| P-M01 | Open worktable | Broad table line beneath an open arch | Office desk or commercial workshop branding |
| P-M02 | Folder and tool | Rounded folder paired with one neutral pencil/ruler form | App-folder icon or sharp tool emphasis |
| P-M03 | Thread loop | One spool and a large loop forming the doorway | Gendered craft stereotype or thin tangled thread |
| P-M04 | Camera frame | Rounded viewfinder frame with one simple circle | Shutter button/action implication or brand mimicry |
| P-M05 | Brush and page | Broad brush crossing one blank sheet | Fine-art prestige or paint splash clutter |
| P-M06 | Repair arc | One rounded wrench-like curve around a simple part | Industrial warning sign or masculine “garage” trope |
| P-M07 | Puzzle join | Two broad interlocking shapes inside a frame | Child puzzle styling or gamified achievement |
| P-M08 | Archive box | Open storage box with one rising document shape | Inbox/download icon or task-complete checkmark |

**Recommended neutral mark:** P-M01. The assistant may select P-M03–P-M08
only when the person explicitly names a compatible activity.

## Direct-browse symbol kit

Generate: **Plans**, **Reference images**, **Notes**, **Materials**, **Drafts**,
**Things to do**, **Recently used** and **Archive**. These symbols organize
content; they do not start tools, purchase materials or claim completion.

## Generation notes

Use the stated project to select objects. Do not fill a generic Projects room
with every hobby at once. No real family photographs, copyrighted artwork,
brand tools, dangerous open blades, fake progress boards, deadlines, shopping
prices or “productivity” scores. Avoid assigning sewing, repair, painting or
technology by gender or age. The neutral Open Worktable must support any
project when no specific visual pack is appropriate.
