# Kitchen room asset dossier

## Room definition

**Job:** keep recipes, shopping lists, cooking plans and appliance notes close
while preserving a direct route to the original material. Kitchen should feel
practical and lived-in, without assigning domestic labor, cuisine, household
role or skill level to the person.

**Suggested purpose copy:** “Recipes, lists and cooking plans.”

## Eight room-mark ideas

The mark is a doorplate emblem shown beside the written **Kitchen** label. It
must read at 64–96px and in one color.

| ID | Mark concept | Silhouette and meaning | Avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| K-M01 | Open cupboard | Rounded doorway containing one shelf and bowl | Multiple shelves or miniature pantry detail |
| K-M02 | Bowl and steam | Broad bowl with two rising curves that form an arch | Restaurant logo styling or hot-food warning cues |
| K-M03 | Herb window | Simple window frame with one two-leaf sprig | Wellness branding or delicate botanical line work |
| K-M04 | Plate on shelf | Circle resting on one horizontal shelf inside a soft frame | Plate-stack detail or formal dining crest |
| K-M05 | Board and handle | Rounded chopping-board outline with one clear handle gap | Knife imagery or sharp corners |
| K-M06 | Whisk loop | One oversized whisk loop contained by a rounded frame | Thin wires that disappear at small size |
| K-M07 | Kettle profile | Friendly low kettle silhouette with simple handle arch | Vintage appliance branding or steam as alarm |
| K-M08 | Table setting | One plate and cup reduced to two strong shapes | Romantic dinner assumptions or multiple place settings |

**Recommended neutral mark:** K-M01. **Expressive alternative:** K-M03.

## Direct-browse symbol kit

Generate one consistent outlined symbol set: **Recipes**, **Shopping lists**,
**Meal plans**, **Favorites**, **Recently used**, **Appliance notes**,
**Ingredients** and **Unfiled**. Use written labels in UI. No symbol may look
like an action to buy, cook, start a timer or turn on an appliance.

## Generation notes

Use broad domestic forms, matte materials, safe counter edges and generous
empty space. Art may suggest light, ceramic, linen, wood or herbs, while the
Harbour Blue product shell retains all semantic controls. Generate no readable
packaging, appliance logos, flames, knives as focal objects, dirty dishes,
alcohol emphasis or culturally assumed cuisine. Kitchen must still read when
the decor layer is hidden.
