# Garden room asset dossier

## Room definition

**Job:** keep plant notes, photographs, seasonal plans and supplies findable.
Garden may mean a balcony pot, indoor plants, community plot or large garden;
the room must not assume property ownership, mobility or a particular climate.

**Suggested purpose copy:** “Plants, seasonal plans and garden notes.”

## Eight room-mark ideas

| ID | Mark concept | Silhouette and meaning | Avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| G-M01 | Open gate leaf | Rounded gate frame with one strong leaf | Detailed fence or private-property cue |
| G-M02 | Watering arch | Watering-can body whose handle forms the room arch | Fine spray dots or product silhouette |
| G-M03 | Potted seedling | Broad pot and two-leaf seedling | Wellness logo or fragile thin stem |
| G-M04 | Garden rows | Three broad rows inside a soft horizon frame | Farm/business scale or crop yield chart |
| G-M05 | Trellis | Simple three-bar trellis with one climbing curve | Dense lattice that fails at small size |
| G-M06 | Sun over plot | Half sun above one planted bed | Weather app badge or hot-weather warning |
| G-M07 | Trowel and leaf | Rounded trowel paired with one leaf | Sharp tool emphasis or hardware-store logo |
| G-M08 | Birdhouse opening | Broad birdhouse doorway with one perch line | Cartoon bird character or surveillance-camera look |

**Recommended neutral mark:** G-M03. **Spatial alternative:** G-M01.

## Direct-browse symbol kit

Generate: **Plants**, **Watering**, **Seasonal plans**, **Photos**, **Seeds**,
**Supplies**, **Things to do** and **Garden journal**. The watering symbol does
not imply an automatic reminder; task state and dates remain written UI.

## Generation notes

Avoid grand estates, compulsory gardening nostalgia, pesticides, sharp tools
as focal objects, exotic-species claims, weather forecasts or fake plant
diagnosis. Plants are broad generic forms unless content identifies a real
plant. Keep pollen-heavy or visually dense florals away from the reading field.
