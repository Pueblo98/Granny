# Fitness and Movement room asset dossier

## Room definition

**Job:** make chosen movement routines, walking plans, class notes and equipment
references easy to resume. The room represents activity without measuring the
person's body, prescribing treatment or suggesting that everyone wants a gym.

**Suggested purpose copy:** “Movement plans, routines and activity notes.”

## Eight room-mark ideas

| ID | Mark concept | Silhouette and meaning | Avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| F-M01 | Mat sunrise | Rolled mat below one broad rising arc | Yoga-only spiritual styling or tiny sun rays |
| F-M02 | Balanced weights | Two simple rounded weights balanced on one line | Heavy bodybuilding or trophy imagery |
| F-M03 | Walking path | Two broad path curves meeting a horizon | Step-count badge or navigation-pin symbol |
| F-M04 | Band and leaf | One resistance-band loop around a two-leaf form | Medical rehabilitation badge |
| F-M05 | Rhythm curve | Two confident movement waves inside a circle | Music-player waveform or pulse monitor |
| F-M06 | Pool lane | Three broad horizontal ripple/lane bands | Warning waves or competitive medal shape |
| F-M07 | Wheel arc | One wheel arc and short ground line | Bicycle brand mark or speed lines |
| F-M08 | Chair movement | Stable chair profile with one open movement curve | Disability/frailty stereotype or hospital chair |

**Recommended neutral mark:** F-M01. **Expressive alternative:** F-M05.

## Direct-browse symbol kit

Generate: **Routines**, **Walking**, **Classes**, **Strength**, **Stretching**,
**Cycling**, **Swimming** and **Equipment notes**. These are content categories,
not performance badges. Every symbol has a written label and may be hidden when
unused.

## Generation notes

No scales, calories, bodies, transformation imagery, heart-rate numbers,
streaks, trophies or idealized fitness models. Avoid hospital physiotherapy,
surveillance mirrors, locker-room imagery and intimidating commercial-gym
density. A chair, mat or light weight is an available object, not a claim about
ability. Real activity data belongs to semantic UI, never the background art.
