# Open Questions

## Product and users

- OQ-001: Which user segment, living context, country/language, and family situation define the first market?
- OQ-002: Which 3–5 workflows constitute MVP, and what evidence would validate the thesis?
- OQ-003: What separates V1 from the full vision, especially memory, family support, and proactivity?
- OQ-004: Which current user behaviors and workarounds have been observed rather than assumed?

## Trust, safety, and data

- OQ-005: Which actions are Level 3, delegated, or restricted, and what confirmation mechanism is valid for each?
- OQ-006: What data is retained, where, for how long, under what consent, and with what deletion/export semantics?
- OQ-007: What may a trusted helper administer or observe, and how does the user see/revoke access?
- OQ-008: How are coercion, impersonation, prompt injection, stolen devices, and account recovery handled?

## Experience and accessibility

- OQ-009: Wake word, physical/software talk button, or both?
- OQ-010: Which internal type, target-size, contrast, timing, caption, speech, and motion requirements should exceed platform baselines?
- OQ-011: What initiative level should be the default, and how do quiet hours/context work?

## Technology and hardware

- OQ-012: What is the exact reference-tablet model/region/OS and its measured accessibility/device-control behavior?
- OQ-013: Which components run on-device versus remotely, including offline fallback?
- OQ-014: Which model/provider, data store, identity, and sync decisions can wait until experiments provide constraints?
- OQ-015: What measured limitation, if any, would justify managed-device or AOSP work?

## Inputs

- OQ-016: Is there an original downloadable/exported planning-conversation artifact to preserve verbatim, beyond the in-chat text captured in the structured source note?
