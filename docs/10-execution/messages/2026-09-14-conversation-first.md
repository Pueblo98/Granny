# Feature-button Home rejected

## Context and evidence

Source: Simon's explicit feedback on 2026-09-14 after reviewing the browser prototype: he dislikes the UI and implementation direction, finds the many buttons inconsistent with a low-learning-burden experience, and wants something more conversation/chatbot-like with built-in functionality. This is product-owner feedback, **not research proving that all older adults prefer chat**.

Expected: express an intent without learning a feature menu. Actual: Home presents five feature shortcuts, Talk/Type, History/Settings and reviewer surfaces; typed entry still requires manual task-category selection.

## Requested response

First finish the development cockpit. Then propose a conversation-led interface with progressive disclosure, embedded results and minimal persistent controls. Preserve explicit microphone activation, silent/touch access, exact consequential confirmations and visible Stop. Do not rewrite the UI until Simon reviews the requested plan.

## Resolution

Simon authorized the [conversation-first build](../../02-design/conversation-first-plan.md) on 2026-09-14, then answered “Continue this direction” after the working message checkpoint on 2026-09-15. This resolves the request to review direction before expansion. [Build record](../sessions/2026-09-14-conversation-build.md) owns integrated delivery and remaining design review. Neither the planning artifact nor this resolution accepts final branding, all interaction details or a production gate.
