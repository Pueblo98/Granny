# Current Milestone — Product and Design Foundation

## Outcome

Establish a navigable single source of truth and prepare the product/design specifications needed to begin Figma exploration and controlled technical prototyping.

## Completed in initialization

- Git/Obsidian-compatible repository foundation and durable agent guidance.
- Preserved Dream Book, handoff, and structured planning-conversation source.
- Canonical vision, product, design, agent, architecture, safety, eval, research, hardware, decision, and execution entry points.
- Six accepted initialization ADRs, concise templates, and proposed design-token schema.
- Seed use cases, journeys, requirements, canonical evals, and failure taxonomy.

## Exit criteria

- Primary proto-persona/job reviewed and initial research plan approved.
- MVP thesis and 3–5 canonical workflows selected.
- Accessibility and voice-UX baselines reviewed against current primary guidance.
- Initial Figma exploration brief accepted.
- Reference tablet physically inventoried and a limited stock-Android feasibility plan defined.

## Out of scope

Substantial Android/backend/agent implementation, AOSP, production infrastructure, final vendor/database choices, final brand palette, and custom dock development.
