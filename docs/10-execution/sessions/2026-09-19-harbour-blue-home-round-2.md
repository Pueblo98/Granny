# Session — Harbour Blue Home composition round 2

## Scope and authority

Simon clarified the active mockup request after round 1: stay entirely on the
Home-load screen, retain the conversation composer at the bottom, compare
multiple-room visualization including scrolling and all-visible approaches,
keep continuation in the option space and restore more free space. This is a
mockup-only round in the primary checkout under the 2026-09-19 exception.

The direction authorizes visual exploration beyond the accepted zero-or-one
room/context Home rule, but does not by itself revise ADR-0014/0015 or authorize
implementation. No room interiors, full Rooms library, production storage,
frontend code, Android build, Figma authoring, branch/PR publication or private
Obsidian changes are in scope. Existing unrelated edits remain preserved.

## What changed

- Added round 2 with four Home-only PNG directions and a 2 × 2 comparison
  sheet. That local intermediate artifact folder is not retained in the
  consolidated checkout.
- Compared a scrolling ribbon, an all-visible field, continuation plus quieter
  room rows and one continuous room horizon. Every direction retains the
  selected Harbour Blue system and bottom Round composer.
- Corrected C's first generated green/yellow/purple UI swatches to Harbour Blue
  line visuals. Regenerated D with smaller room portraits and more Linen space
  after full-size inspection found the first output too full.
- Marked round 1 as superseded for current composition review without deleting
  or rewriting its assets and generation evidence.
- Updated the gallery and milestone. The style-board index remains unchanged
  because this round applies, rather than reopens, the selected palette board.

## Evidence

All four local PNGs were opened at original detail. Required Home copy, four
fictional room names/purposes, written Menu, Type a request, Ask me anything…,
Talk and Send are visible and correctly spelled. A communicates scrolling with
a partial fourth room; B shows all rooms; C includes fictional Vegetable soup
continuation plus three other rooms; D shows four arched thresholds in one
region. No tabs, bottom navigation, room interior, idle Stop, alerts, metrics,
prompt chips or real/private content appears.

The image generator does not certify fonts, exact pixels, dp/sp, focus order or
responsive behavior. Natural room art uses illustrative colors while UI roles
remain Harbour Blue. B and C are intentionally denser stress cases; D leaves
the largest quiet band after correction. The generated comparison resamples
the screens and is not authoritative for fine geometry.

Repository validation and Obsidian-open evidence are recorded at handoff after
the maintenance commands complete.

## Handoff

The round is ready for Simon's local review on `main` under the mockup exception;
there is no commit, push, PR or merge. The next bounded decision is which Home
treatment to refine, or which restrained parts to combine without creating a
busier fifth composition. Room interiors and implementation remain stopped.
