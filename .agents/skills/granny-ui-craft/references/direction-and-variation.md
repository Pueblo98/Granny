# Direction and variation

Use this reference when the work needs a new composition or when repeated UI patterns are the problem.

## Establish the invariant brief

Hold these constant across every direction:

- the same user goal and realistic fictional content;
- the same accepted/proposed behavior and action class;
- the same required Talk, Type, Change, Cancel and Stop access;
- the same success, partial, unknown, permission and failure truth;
- the same minimum target sizes, text scaling and focus semantics;
- the same active fidelity and simulation labels.

If changing a direction would also change one of those facts, separate it as a product-contract proposal before comparison.

## Create structural difference

Produce three directions that differ in at least three consequential dimensions:

1. information sequence and what receives first attention;
2. relationship between conversation, result content and current controls;
3. reading measure, density and use of the tablet canvas;
4. how the active task/status stays visible while history recedes;
5. how one distinctive visual detail reinforces the product's character.

Changing only color, radius, shadow or typeface is a skin, not a new direction. Do not generate a grid of unrelated style presets.

For Granny's conversation surface, useful structural questions include:

- Does the current turn occupy a focused reading column or a context-and-result composition on expanded width?
- Does a message preview remain inline until scale/content demands an expanded reading state?
- How do rich photo or media results feel native to the thread without becoming a dashboard?
- Where can Stop remain stable without making the whole interface feel alarmed?
- How does the invitation teach capability without a permanent feature menu or wall of prompt chips?

## Select deliberately

Evaluate each direction against:

- immediate comprehension of the present task;
- ease of speaking, typing, correcting and continuing;
- exact consequence visibility and protection from incidental approval;
- stable Stop and truthful outcome status;
- 200%+ text, keyboard, TalkBack and non-audio use;
- compact, medium and expanded window behavior;
- adult dignity and avoidance of medical, childish or companion stereotypes;
- specificity: whether the composition would still make sense if the product name were removed.

Recommend one direction and explain the sacrifice it makes. Preserve credible alternatives for review; do not average them into a fourth generic compromise.
