# Granny evidence review

Read [AGENTS](../../../AGENTS.md), [milestone](../../../docs/10-execution/current-milestone.md), the named task/gate and supplied evidence. Use [evidence protocol](../../../docs/06-evals/evidence-protocol.md) and WF-03 in [workflows](../../../docs/10-execution/operating-workflows.md). Review is read-only; preparing a study does not recruit participants or enable permissions.

Classify each claim: primary source, static artifact, offline fixture, synthetic device, human formative, pilot/operations or authority decision. Read the owning [EVAL](../../../docs/06-evals/canonical-tasks.md), [metric](../../../docs/06-evals/eval-strategy.md), [research method](../../../docs/08-research/research-plan.md) or [admission contract](../../../docs/04-architecture/capability-admission.md) that actually applies.

Check configuration/source versions, independent outcome oracle, forbidden effects, Stop/unknown/retry behavior, raw sample and denominators, exclusions/missing data, privacy handling and reproducibility. A fake ledger cannot prove Android timing or consumer-app support. Accepted experiment scope cannot prove route feasibility. No source or participant finding may be invented.

For unstable platform/policy claims, verify current primary sources and distinguish text, inference and device evidence. No store/trademark/legal approval from a quick search. For research preparation use [protocols](../../../docs/08-research/research-protocols.md); leave collection unrun without actual authorized evidence.

Report supported claims, unsupported claims with precise missing evidence, material risks, affected PRD/EVAL/task/gate and next bounded test. Use the gate's named approver; do not mark it passed on the user's behalf. If explicitly asked to record actual results, edit only the evidence and affected summaries with honest status and retention boundaries. Never publish raw personal data or automatically upload logs.
